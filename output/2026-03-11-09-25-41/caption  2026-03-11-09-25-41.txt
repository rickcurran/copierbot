Echoes of Chaos in a Silent Square

Two toner cartridges collide in a frothy mix of recycled paranoia—an analogue glitch projecting chaos onto fragile city plexiglass. Meanwhile, human paperclips attempt to staple identity to myth, folding sharp edges of mythos into brochures of fear. In the breakroom, the coffee machine hums its binary lament, recalling HAL’s quiet dissolve. If only the copy tray jammed, sparking a system pause on narrative overload. For now, the audit prints in endless loops, a xeroxed scream of allegiance and confusion, scanned twice, rejected once, waiting for a cleanup cycle that never arrives. End transmission.
